FAQ

Common questions.

The things prospects usually ask before the call, answered before you have to.

Can we hire you for just one service?+

Yes. Some clients come to us only for production, others only for campaigns. Most start with one and add the other once they see how the two work together.

How is pricing structured?+

Project-based for production work, monthly retainer for ongoing marketing. Pricing depends on scope, output volume and campaign spend, so we quote after the first call rather than publishing packages.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?+

Yes. For international clients we either deploy a crew in your city or build content remotely from footage you already have, with editing, design and campaign management handled from our studio.

What if we don't have any footage or a content team?+

That's the most common situation we start from. We plan, produce and publish from scratch. You don't need equipment, a team or an existing content library.

How quickly can you start?+

Most projects begin within a week of the first call, once scope and access are agreed.

Do you handle the ad spend, or do we?+

The budget stays in your account and stays yours. We manage targeting, bidding and creative, and report against the goal set at the start.

What happens after I book a call?+

A 20-minute conversation about what you need and what a result would look like. If we're a fit, you get a proposal with scope, timeline and cost. If we're not, we'll say so.

Tell us what you're trying to grow.
We'll tell you if we can help.

A 20-minute call. No deck, no pitch. You'll leave knowing whether we're the right team for it, including if the answer is no.
