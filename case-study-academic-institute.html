From zero social presence to 65 enrollments in 30 days.

A private academic institute in Bhimber, Azad Kashmir had no social media presence and was burning cash boosting posts instead of running real campaigns. Here's what changed once content and ads worked together.

ClientPrivate academic institute, Bhimber AJK
TypeSingle-campus school
Timeline~30 days of ads, run in November
Result65 students enrolled

The problem

The institute had no social media presence at all. What ad spend they did have wasn't going toward real campaigns — they were boosting individual Facebook posts, which burns money without any real targeting, tracking or strategy behind it.

The result was predictable: no consistent content, no qualified reach, and enrollment numbers that weren't moving.

What we did

We started with content, not ads. The founder is a well-known, socially active figure in the Bhimber community, so instead of generic school marketing, we built the entire content library around him — his vision for the school, a tour of the facilities (labs and classrooms), and students speaking on camera. His personal credibility became the trust signal the campaign was built on.

Once that content library existed, we moved to paid: Meta ad creative built from the same footage, run through November, targeted at parents in the Bhimber area, focused entirely on admissions.

It took about a month of running ads before results showed up.

The results

122 leads came in through Meta at just PKR 55.65 per lead — and 65 of those converted into actual enrollments within 30 days.
